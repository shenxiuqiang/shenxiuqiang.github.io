V6 changed four things at once: the four roof planes were flattened into a single 5% pitch falling south, the entrance was reduced to one gateway on the central axis, both sides of the skywells became full-height sliding glass, and the kitchen and bathroom were reorganised onto one side each. The images run from *Overview & courtyard* to *Interior & skywells*; click any one to open the full-size render.

- **Engine**: Cycles (Metal, 64 samples)
- **Resolution**: 1600 × 1248
- **Model size**: 1416 objects
- **Blender**: 5.2.2 LTS

The cutaway and plan views hide part of the enclosure as noted in the drawings, and doors are shown open. Structural members and fittings express design intent; construction follows the specialist drawings.
